# Hi, I'm Patricia 👋

Computational biologist in Perth, Australia, studying how microbes and viruses shape early-life health.
I'm part of the **Immunology and Breastfeeding team** at The Kids Research Institute Australia and The University of Western Australia.

🧬 **Research:** respiratory & gut microbiome and virome · human milk · multi-omics integration  
🛠️ **I build:** reproducible Nextflow/nf-core and Snakemake pipelines, and interactive Shiny apps that make omics data easier to explore  
🌎 Colombian in Australia · ¡hablo español!

### 🦠 Viromics pipelines

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**everest_nf**](https://github.com/agudeloromero/everest_nf) | EVEREST, a pipeline for viral assembly and characterisation, rebuilt in Nextflow DSL2 using the nf-core template. Runs with Docker, Singularity or Conda. |
| [**EVEREST_meta**](https://github.com/agudeloromero/EVEREST_meta) | Snakemake version of EVEREST for metagenomics, for virus and phage discovery from paired-end or single-end reads. [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.10487445.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10487445) |
